Private beta

Clear expectations for an early product.

Orbit’s private beta request channel is not open through this website yet. These terms describe the intended boundaries for any invitation-based evaluation.

No public download · No automatic cloud sync · No form submission

Availability

Beta access, if offered, is invitation-based, limited, and may be changed, paused, or ended. This website is not a public download offer and does not guarantee that a request will be accepted.

Your responsibilities

Use Orbit only with information you are entitled to store. Protect your device, any exported backup, and any backup service credential. Do not use Orbit to harass people, violate their privacy, break the law, probe the service, or interfere with other users or systems.

Beta software

Beta software may contain defects, change without notice, or lose data. Make a current backup before testing restore behavior and do not rely on Orbit as the only copy of critical information. No uptime, availability, or data-recovery promise is made for the beta.

Not professional advice

Orbit is a personal relationship reminder tool. It does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, safety, or emergency advice, and it should not be used as a substitute for professional judgment or emergency services.

Support boundaries

A monitored public support channel and response-time commitment have not been announced. The current support guide provides self-service steps. Additional beta terms may be provided with an invitation and will take precedence if they conflict with this page.
