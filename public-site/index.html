A calmer check-in loop

Remember the context. Reach out with intention.

Orbit brings the details and timing for personal follow-ups into one focused place on your device.

01

Remember more than a name

Keep the notes, relationship details, birthdays, tags, and interaction history you choose to add.

02

Set a gentle cadence

Choose when to check in, then use due, overdue, upcoming, snooze, pause, and archive states to keep the list realistic.

03

Keep the core local-first

Relationship data is stored on your device by default. Optional contact import is read-only and reminders are local notifications.

Local-first, not cloud-dependent

Your relationship graph stays on your device by default.

Orbit does not provide automatic cloud sync. An advanced, manual backup and restore workflow can be configured separately; backup files contain sensitive relationship data, and its service credentials require careful handling.
